COMPUTER ENGINEERING AT UE

THE BS CpE PROGRAM AT THE UNIVERSITY OF THE EAST

📖 PROGRAM OVERVIEW

The BS Computer Engineering (CpE) program at the University of the East trains students to understand both sides of the hardware-software interface, from designing circuits to building operating systems. It draws on both electrical engineering and computer science, preparing graduates to design and develop embedded digital and computer systems for modern industries, including the games industry.

The program centers on applying theory through semestral and year-long projects, with students taking on increasing creative control as they advance and receiving feedback from their instructors along the way.

🎯 PROGRAM OBJECTIVES

UE's CpE program aims to produce graduates who are:

  • Professionally competent and globally competitive in Computer Engineering
  • Equipped with technical skills that contribute to the country's social and economic progress
  • Lifelong learners guided by UE's core values of excellence, integrity, professionalism, teamwork, commitment, transparency, accountability, and social responsibility

🛠️ SKILLS DEVELOPED

Applying math and science to solve complex engineering problems

Designing systems and components within real-world constraints

Working effectively on multidisciplinary teams

Using modern engineering tools and techniques

Communicating clearly, in writing and in person

Recognizing professional and ethical responsibility

🎓 LEARNING EXPERIENCES

Freshman and sophomore courses build a foundation in hardware, software, math, physics, and chemistry, preparing students for the design-focused courses of junior and senior year. Lectures are paired with laboratory work, where students design, simulate, and analyze a project before building and testing it by hand.

By the time students reach Microprocessor System Design and their Senior Project, they combine both hardware and software skills, including writing their own operating system code and designing hardware components such as memory controllers.

🔬 LABORATORIES & FOCUS AREAS

Coursework and lab work in the CpE program center on embedded systems, the microprocessor-driven components found inside everyday devices such as game consoles, robots, digital cameras, and even aircraft systems. Students also choose from electives in networks, embedded systems, VHDL, digital signal processing, VLSI, control systems, and artificial intelligence.

🚀 STUDENT PROJECTS

The Senior Project brings together everything students have learned in a two-part capstone: teamwork, technical design, report writing, and oral presentation. It's meant to mirror the real challenges of working as an engineer, combining solid hardware and software skills built up over the whole curriculum.

🏆 RESEARCH & ACADEMIC OPPORTUNITIES

UE CpE students and faculty have been recognized in national competitions and awards, including finishes in the ICpEP.se NCR Quiz Bowl and recognitions such as the GMA Network Awardee in the Technology-based Category. The program is under the UE College of Engineering, which holds PAASCU Level III Re-Accreditation.

Graduates go on to roles such as project engineer, systems analyst, network administrator, software engineer, and technopreneur, with career paths spanning technology areas like robotics, AI, telecommunications, and control systems.
